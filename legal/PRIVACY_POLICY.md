## 4. Information visible to other users

Other league participants can see information needed to participate, including display/team names, draft selections, rosters, trades and standings. Commissioners can access the membership and league-management information their role permits.

Discoverable league information can appear in search or previews. This can include league names, commissioner display names and member/team display information. Visibility depends on the feature and league settings; do not put personal information you want kept private into names or other shared fields.

Your password and authentication tokens are not public profile fields. Your account email is used for account and service purposes rather than as a public display name.
